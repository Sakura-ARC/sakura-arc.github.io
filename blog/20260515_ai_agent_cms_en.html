AI Updates My Site Faster Than SNS Posts!
—— Antigravity×GitHub Pages

By Haru Minamo 2026-05-15

Preface: Why This Blog is Neither Note nor WordPress

Actually, this article was completed using only chat commands. The AI conceived the article, generated the HTML, pushed it to GitHub, and completed the publication. All I did was "convey the direction" and "confirm the result."

Why did I choose to start a blog on such a static site instead of Note, WordPress, or X (formerly Twitter)? The answer is simple: with the AI agent, updating your own site is easier than posting on SNS. And there's one more reason: your own site's content accumulates as SEO assets—a low-key but steady long-term strategy.

【Experience】The "Fatigue" of SNS Posting and the Introduction of Antigravity

In 2026, using AI to write code or articles is no longer special. Anyone can get decent results just by opening a web app like ChatGPT or Claude.

However, for individual developers without an IT background, the biggest barrier is not the AI's "answer" itself, but the "friction" in reflecting those answers into their own projects.

Looking back, environments for using GitHub Copilot or Gemini within VSCode were already mature since around last year (2025). Even when developing with a game engine like Ren'Py, the experience of AI suggesting the continuation of code was certainly convenient.

But that's just "assistance within the editor." Creating files yourself, copy-pasting the AI's answer, saving, managing with Git, deploying... the invisible wall between the AI and local files was still high for IT laymen, and more than anything, it was tedious.

The turning point came between February and March 2026. I introduced the AI agent "Antigravity" during the novel writing process for the Hayakawa SF Contest.

What I realized there was the "ability to maintain context" that never exists in web app versions. No need to copy-paste past materials one by one, and the AI never loses sight of the big picture. The AI freely walks around my repository, reads materials, and updates configuration drafts.

"It's great that the AI can read, create, and update materials and scripts within my own PC. It's scary though lol"

Even more innovative is the fact that this agent can "operate a browser (Chrome) just like a human." Not just searching the net, but if necessary, it launches a browser itself, crawls sites, retrieves information, and integrates it into the project. This is a realm unique to agents with "hands and feet," impossible for web app versions of AI.

Of course, this isn't a "magic bullet" that solves everything. AI context maintenance still has limits, and human "rein-handling"—giving appropriate instructions each time and having it read necessary materials—is essential. I also use various AIs like web app versions and NotebookLM according to the purpose.

However, by placing Antigravity at the "center" of the project, the vast amount of time previously spent on copy-paste work has undoubtedly shifted toward more essential trial and error.

【Discovery】The Reversal: Updating Own Site is "Easier" Than SNS

After submitting the novel, I moved to the project for the mystery game "Kamiya Detective Agency" currently in production, and a surprise awaited me there as well.

Normally, mainstream information dissemination happens on Note or X. However, for someone like me who is not good at writing, the task of facing an external platform's posting form and copy-pasting and formatting the AI's answer was surprisingly stressful.

But in the Antigravity ✕ GitHub Pages environment? Just chat with the AI: "Write a post about my thoughts on this development and publish it with the usual template." The AI directly generates HTML, organizes directories, pushes it, and publishes it to the world.

"Updating my own site with AI is overwhelmingly easier than copy-pasting to external SNS." This incredible reversal occurred.

【Essence】Why GitHub Pages ✕ AI is the Ultimate Combination

Why GitHub Pages now? The reason lies in the "simplicity" of this platform, which perfectly aligns with the characteristics of AI agents.

When trying to leave traditional hosting or CMS (like WordPress) to an AI, complex steps like logging in, form entry, DB management, or FTP transfer become barriers. These are designed assuming "humans operate via a browser," making them difficult for AI to handle and carrying a high risk of breaking things.

However, the act of "publishing" on GitHub Pages is unified into just one point: "Git push."

Creating files, placing them in folders, committing, and pushing. For an AI agent, this is an extension of its most adept "file operations." As a result of extremely high abstraction of operations, "editing a repository" has become a far safer and faster deployment method for AI than "hitting a management screen."

All we have to do is give a command in chat: "Update this article." The AI handles the writing behind the scenes, generates images, formats HTML, and completes the push. The routine of "blog updating," which used to be a chore, disappears from consciousness. This is the true identity of the "ultimate publishing infrastructure" for individual development brought by the combination of AI Agent ✕ GitHub Pages.

Of course, it's not all rosy. For example, multilingual support for the Steam store was a "copy-paste hell" despite having master data for 18 languages in JSON, because of the vast amount of input items on the UI that aren't included in the JSON. While this might be automated using Antigravity's browser operation function, the psychological brake on the human side—feeling "scared" to leave the heart of the project, like store settings or X posts, entirely to an AI—is part of the real development landscape today.

【SEO】Own Site Content Accumulates as "Assets"

There is also a hidden intention in operating a blog on a static site: content on your own site directly becomes an SEO asset.

Posts on Note or X can disappear at any time due to platform circumstances. If the algorithm changes, exposure drops sharply, and if the service stops, everything is lost. However, content accumulated under your own domain gains higher trust from Google over time and becomes a stable entry point through search.

This article itself is the first step of that experiment. If it gradually starts getting caught in searches for keywords like "AI Agent," "GitHub Pages," and "Individual Development"... that's a slow but steady accumulation strategy. I plan to detail deep topics like Steam review handling and 18-language localization in subsequent articles.

【Conclusion】AI Becomes a "Co-developer of the Project"

The fact that I, without specialized IT knowledge, can perform game production in 18 languages and lightning-fast construction of modern websites is entirely because I can thoroughly use AI not just as "assistance" but as the "ultimate tool that shares the project and handles concrete practical work."

Of course, there is risk and fear in letting AI freely touch files on your PC. Human "rein-handling," such as managing history on GitHub or taking backups across different systems, is necessary. However, the sense of speed gained here more than makes up for that fear. AI has broken down technical barriers, and what I concentrate on now is only the decision of "how to make the project more interesting next." If you're tired of copy-pasting with AI, or hesitating because you're an "IT layman," I strongly recommend inviting an agent into your repository right now. A future of individual development that is freer than you imagine and "not lonely" awaits you there.

"Though you might get lonely if you rely too much on AI lol"
