Introduction: This place is an archive for recording the activities of the individual indie studio "Sakura ARC," as well as the knowledge and thoughts gained during the production process.

In the lonely journey of game production, what am I thinking, what am I resisting, and where am I headed with my new partner, AI? I intend to record these fragments with a touch like that of a news media outlet.

A Return to "Hard-Boiled"

What I am trying to create is a mystery with a dry atmosphere. I want to reconstruct the "aesthetic of not over-explaining" that former PC adventure games and hard-boiled novels possessed, using modern technology.

Stepping away from excessive direction and kind-hearted design that overly pampers the player, I aim for an experience that values the "steady footwork" of investigating with one's own feet and deducing with one's own head.

AI is a "Brush" and a "Co-author"

In the production of this work, the existence of AI (especially large language models) is indispensable. However, it is not "to make things easy with automatic generation." It's quite the opposite. I am utilizing it as a powerful amplifier to surpass "individual limits," such as the depth of expression and multilingual development in 18 languages that I could never have reached alone.

Interacting with AI, sometimes being contradicted, and sometimes being surprised while weaving a story. I am convinced that this process itself is a new "form of creation" in the modern era.

"So that the wall of technology does not become a wall of passion."

Afterword: An Endless Experiment

To be honest, I don't have any future prospects or noble principles. I'm just purely enjoying the "experiment" itself—making a game alone with AI as a partner, or building this site itself.

By the way, mentioning the names of "Gnosia" and "Ace Attorney" in the first half is just a facade written by AI under the instruction "Let's put the names of masterpieces for SEO purposes!"

...But, well, it is an undeniable fact that I have been seriously and crazily enjoying them.

Today (May 13, 2026), I successfully submitted the Steam store page for this work, "Kamiya Detective Agency: High School Girl Vanished in The Summer" for review. With a lighthearted stance that I'd be lucky if it hits once on Steam, I intend to continue giving shape to what I want to create at my own pace.
